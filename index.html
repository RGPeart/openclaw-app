OpenClaw

A personal assistant that works inside one person's own Google account.

What it does

OpenClaw is a private, self-hosted assistant run by its owner, Ryan Peart, for his own use. With the owner's permission it can:

  • read and search the owner's Gmail, and draft or send email only after the owner has approved the exact message;
  • read and create events on the owner's Google Calendar;
  • read, create and edit the owner's Google Docs, Sheets, Slides and Drive files.

Who can use it

OpenClaw is not a public service. It has one user, the owner, who signs in to his own Google account. It does not offer sign-in to anyone else.

Your data

Google data accessed by OpenClaw is processed on the owner's own private server. To complete a task the owner requested, excerpts may be sent to an AI model provider. The data is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else for their own use. See the Privacy Policy for details.

OpenClaw is a personal project and has not been through Google's app verification. Access: Gmail (read and send), Google Calendar, and Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides.
